# Hi, I'm Sebastian

I'm an engineering leader at dubizzle in Dubai. Most of my work, and most of what I write about, sits in one place: getting AI out of pilots and into things people actually use.

## What I'm working on

- **Making AI work in production.** I care less about what a model can do in a demo and more about what happens when it's wrong, who finds out, and what the system does when it isn't sure.
- **Research on new model architectures.** I'm testing ideas around state-space (Mamba-style) models and running language models on a phone, with the goal of finding out what actually works rather than what sounds good.
- **Leading engineering teams at scale.**

## What I believe

- Measure before you build. Test an idea against past cases before writing the system.
- A good AI system has a plan for being unsure: it stops and hands over instead of guessing.
- Negative results are results. I measure an idea, and if it fails I say so and drop it. Several of my own ideas, and several products I built, are the evidence, and I've written those retrospectives up honestly.
